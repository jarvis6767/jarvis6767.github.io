Privacy Policy

This app (jarvis.appolo) is a personal, individually-owned project. It only accesses the TikTok account of its own owner, using TikTok's official API, to post video content on that owner's behalf.

No data collected through this integration is shared with, sold to, or accessed by any third party. Access tokens are stored locally and used only to communicate with TikTok's API.

Contact: the app owner via their TikTok developer account.
